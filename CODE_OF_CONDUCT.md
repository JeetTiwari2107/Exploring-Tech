# Code of Conduct

LocalGit Studio welcomes constructive participation from people with different experience, backgrounds, and perspectives.

Participants are expected to be respectful, considerate, and focused on the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation that violates these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the repository's contact channel; do not include personal or sensitive information in a public issue.
